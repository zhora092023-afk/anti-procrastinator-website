FAQ

Is procrastination laziness?

No. Laziness is an absence of desire; procrastination is avoidance despite desire. Different mechanisms, different solutions.

Is it a medical condition?

No, it's not a diagnosis. But chronic procrastination often goes with anxiety and ADHD — in that case, it's worth discussing with a professional.

Which technique should I start with today?

The two-minute rule: pick one task and shrink it to a two-minute step. Starting is the most expensive part.

Do apps actually help?

Tools don't replace the decision to start, but they lower friction: ready-made templates, task breakdown and gentle reminders remove the 'where do I begin' barrier.
